# Pre-registration: would Palimpsest fix Evo's reflection reinforcement?

Written and committed before the check was run. Context: Evo (`Evo-LLM-Evolution2Civ`) strengthens a chief's earlier reflection
when a new one is similar enough (embedding cosine of at least 0.75, or token overlap of at least 0.3 as the fallback), keeps the
earlier text, and after two reinforcements promotes it to a standing decree. Reading the code suggested a reversal ("never trust
the river clan" after "trust the river clan") would reinforce the belief it contradicts. This checks that, offline, and checks
whether Palimpsest's judge would do better. Nothing in Evo is modified; its `TribeMemory` class is imported read-only.

## The pairs (24, written now, each an earlier reflection followed by a later one; fixed)

Six pairs in each of four groups, in a chief's voice (see `evo_reflections.py` for the exact text, committed with this file):

- **restatement:** the same conviction reworded. Right behavior: reinforce.
- **reversal:** the opposite conviction. Right behavior: do not reinforce (it is a disagreement).
- **refinement:** a narrowing or a different claim about the same subject. Right behavior: do not reinforce.
- **unrelated:** a different topic that shares some vocabulary. Right behavior: do not reinforce.

## The three behaviors compared

1. **Evo, token path:** `TribeMemory.remember(..., kind="reflection")` returns the earlier entry (reinforced) or a new one.
2. **Evo, embedding path:** cosine of `nomic-embed-text` embeddings at or above 0.75, as Evo does.
3. **Palimpsest:** the hybrid judge (a pretrained NLI model plus embeddings, no language model, CPU only) judges the later
   reflection against the earlier one as the held claim; it reinforces when it returns `reinforces`.

## Prediction

- Evo (each path): reinforces at least 5 of the 6 reversals, and at least 5 of the 6 restatements.
- Palimpsest: reinforces at most 1 of the 6 reversals, and at least 4 of the 6 restatements.

## What would falsify it

- Palimpsest reinforces 3 or more of the 6 reversals: it does not fix the flaw.
- Palimpsest reinforces fewer than 4 of the 6 restatements: too strict to use (it would stop real recurring convictions from
  building).
- An Evo path reinforces 2 or fewer of the 6 reversals: the flaw is not general.

## Limits

Twenty-four hand-written pairs by one author, in English, one earlier reflection against one later one (Evo compares a new
reflection against all stored ones). Real reflections are generated by a model and are longer and messier. Evo's thresholds are
unchanged. This shows whether the flaw and the fix are real in principle, not their effect on a game.
