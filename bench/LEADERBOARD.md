# Leaderboard

Generated by `bench/run.py --report`. Held-out cases only: dev cases are for tuning and never ranked. Scoring and labels: [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md), [CASES.md](CASES.md). `unsafe` is the share of true conflicts the contender failed to flag (the error that matters most); `false alarm` is the share of non-conflicts it flagged; `wrong replacement` is how often it overwrote a belief that should have stayed; `missed change` is how often a replacement or exception went unrecognized and left a stale belief standing.

No contender has been scored on the current held-out cases yet. The held-out batch (v2) was written after the first batch's errors were read, and frozen before any change made in response to them. The previous board, on the first batch of cases, is kept in [results/leaderboard-cases-v1.md](results/leaderboard-cases-v1.md).
